About Me

Hands-on mobile engineering, technical ownership, and direct client collaboration.

I’ve been building Android applications professionally since 2015, across fintech, e-commerce, healthcare, logistics, retail, and consumer products. My work ranges from creating new applications to modernizing legacy code and delivering changes in mature production systems, including a fintech app serving more than 500,000 active users during my engagement.

As Senior Android Engineer & Owner at Bitwoo, I lead mobile engineering and client delivery. I combine hands-on development and architecture with requirements clarification, technical planning, estimates, code review, QA, and release coordination, working directly with clients throughout delivery.

My core stack includes Kotlin, Java, Jetpack Compose, Kotlin Multiplatform, and Compose Multiplatform, supported by Clean Architecture, MVVM, Coroutines / Flow, Firebase, Supabase, Room, REST APIs, and unit testing.

My multiplatform experience includes Bitwoo’s Nexus Gym product, shipped for Android and iOS with Compose Multiplatform, and technical supervision and product definition for Venus, a gym-management MVP with shared Kotlin logic, Jetpack Compose on Android, and SwiftUI on iOS.

I also use AI-assisted development with human review and pull requests, retaining responsibility for technical decisions and integration.
